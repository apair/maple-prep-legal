Privacy Policy

Effective August 30, 2026

MaplePrep is an independent study application for Canadian citizenship-test preparation. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada or another government agency.

Information processed by the app

Most study data is stored only on the learner’s device. This can include test-date preferences, province, study language and goal, diagnostic and practice results, lesson history, mastery estimates, XP, streaks, reminder preferences, and an interrupted study session.

An account is optional. If a learner requests cloud backup, MaplePrep processes the email address used for passwordless sign-in, an account identifier, and the study progress selected for synchronization. Screen location and device-specific notification schedule identifiers are not uploaded with cloud progress.

If the AI Tutor backend is configured and a learner requests AI help, the app sends a question identifier, selected choice identifier, requested explanation mode, and requested language to MaplePrep’s backend. The backend rebuilds factual context from MaplePrep’s reviewed question bank and sends that context to OpenAI to produce the explanation. The app does not send an email address with a Tutor request. Tutor requests use stateless API processing (store: false) in MaplePrep’s implementation.

If subscriptions are configured, Apple, Google, and RevenueCat process purchase and entitlement information. MaplePrep does not receive or store full payment-card details.

If privacy-safe product analytics is configured, the app sends a small allowlisted set of events such as completion counts, bounded scores, fixed feature or paywall reason codes, booleans, and timestamps. Analytics events do not contain names, email addresses, account or device identifiers, selected answers, question text, Tutor content, test dates, provinces, or notification identifiers. Hosting and network providers may still process ordinary technical information such as IP addresses in service logs.

How information is used

Information is used to:

  • provide lessons, practice, readiness estimates, reminders, and interrupted-session recovery;
  • synchronize progress when a learner chooses cloud backup;
  • provide requested AI explanations;
  • validate and restore subscriptions;
  • understand aggregate beta reliability and feature completion; and
  • prevent abuse and protect the service.

MaplePrep does not sell personal information or use study data for third-party behavioural advertising.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Device data remains until it is cleared by the operating system, the app is uninstalled, or the app provides a specific clearing action. Cloud progress remains until the learner deletes the cloud account. The in-app Delete cloud account action permanently deletes the authentication account and its cloud progress; study progress already stored on that device remains locally. Store purchase records are retained by the relevant app store and RevenueCat according to their legal and operational requirements.

Operational logs and queued anonymous analytics are retained only as needed for security, delivery, debugging, and aggregate beta analysis. The analytics queue is bounded on the device.

Service providers

Depending on configured features, MaplePrep may use:

  • Supabase for optional authentication and cloud progress;
  • OpenAI for requested AI Tutor explanations;
  • RevenueCat, Apple, and Google for subscriptions; and
  • the selected hosting provider for serverless endpoints and privacy-safe analytics delivery.

Each provider processes information under its own terms and privacy commitments.

Security

MaplePrep uses passwordless authentication, per-user database access policies, authenticated account deletion, server-only administrative credentials, and encrypted network connections provided by platform services. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Children

MaplePrep is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact the developer so it can be removed.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be reflected by updating this policy and its effective date. For a privacy request, open a private-information-free support request. Do not include passwords, purchase credentials, or sensitive personal information.
